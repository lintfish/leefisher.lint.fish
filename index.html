About Lee

Lee Fisher is an ex-police motorcyclist based in Bo’ness. He is a former IAM RoadSmart car and bike examiner, with the IMI examiner qualification, and an IAM Masters Mentor. He rides a Kawasaki Versys.

He offers one-to-one post-test advanced coaching in the police riding system, in a Roadcraft style. It is the paid next step after Police Scotland’s Rider Refinement or an IAM course — built around you, your bike, and the roads of Central Scotland.

This business is not affiliated with or endorsed by Police Scotland or IAM RoadSmart.
