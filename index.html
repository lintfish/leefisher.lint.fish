About Lee

Lee Fisher is an ex-police motorcyclist based in Bo’ness. He recently retired from the Police Scotland Central Road Traffic unit. He is a former IAM RoadSmart car and bike examiner, with the IMI examiner qualification, and an IAM Masters Mentor. He rides a Kawasaki Versys.

He offers one-to-one post-test advanced coaching in the police riding system, in a Roadcraft style — the paid next step after Police Scotland’s Rider Refinement or an IAM course. Coaching is built around several short sessions at intervals, with time to practise in between, which suits time-poor riders: new licence holders, back-to-biking riders, and riders who want to upskill. He covers West Lothian and the Central Belt.

How it works

Lee’s coaching is built around several short sessions at intervals, with time to practise in between. That suits time-poor riders — new licence holders, back-to-biking riders, and riders who want to upskill.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Call or text to talk through what you want from the session. Booking is by phone or text for now; online booking is coming soon.

  2. 02

    Agree a meeting point

    Lee is based in Bo’ness and covers West Lothian and the Central Belt. Queensferry is the meeting point for riders coming from the north.

  3. 03

    A typical session

    It starts with a normal ride so Lee can gauge your ability. Then comes a coaching ride with guidance over the intercom, plus debriefs at stops.

  4. 04

    Practise and come back

    Book further hours or a pack when you are ready, leaving time between sessions to put the work into practice.
